# CodeAlpha_PhishingAwarenessTraining

Task 2 of the CodeAlpha Cyber Security Internship, by Ayorinde Ashogbon: an interactive online module called **Personal Cybersecurity Awareness Assessment**, focused on phishing, social engineering and deepfake attacks.

## What it covers
- **What phishing is:** email, spear phishing, whaling and BEC, smishing, vishing, quishing, clone sites, and deepfakes and AI phishing
- **Spot the email:** a fictional bank email where you tap each red flag to see why it gives the scam away
- **Fake websites:** an address dissector that highlights who really owns a web address, plus lookalike, padlock and `@` tricks
- **Social engineering tactics:** urgency, authority, fear, reward, curiosity, familiarity, and AI voice and video cloning
- **Real-world cases:** Podesta email (2016), Twitter staff phishing (2020), Google and Facebook fake invoices, Target breach (2013), Arup deepfake video call (2024), each with a source link
- **Best practices:** a 9-habit checklist and steps to take if you already clicked
- **Quiz:** 9 questions with instant feedback and a score

## How to use
Open `phishing-training.html` in any modern browser. No install, server or internet connection is needed except for the web fonts and the source links. Work through the sections in order, then take the quiz.

## Notes
- Everything runs in the browser. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
- "Northbank" and every address ending in `.example` are fictional and used only for teaching.
- Case summaries are brief. Read the linked sources for full details.

## Ethical use
This module is for education and awareness only. Do not use it to build or send real phishing messages.
